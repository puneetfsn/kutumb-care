## Kutumb Care — trial build (debug APK)

**Your family's emergency dossier — 100% private, 100% offline.**
Kutumb Care records *where* your family's important documents and accounts live — Aadhaar, PAN, passports, bank accounts, FDs/RDs, insurance, EPF, property, education and medical documents — so the right person can find them in an emergency. Nothing ever leaves the phone: no internet, no account, no cloud, no ads.

### 📲 Install (2 minutes)
1. Download **kutumb-care-….apk** from the Assets below and open it.
2. Android will warn about installing from outside the Play Store — choose **Install anyway**. (Trial builds are distributed directly until the Play Store launch.)
3. On first open, choose **English / हिन्दी**, set a passphrase (6+ characters) if you wish, and start with the records on the home screen.

🌐 **No Android phone handy?** Use the web version in any browser: https://puneetfsn.github.io/kutumb-care/ — add it to your Home Screen and it works offline like an app.

### What's inside
- **52 ready-made records** across 9 sections: identity, banking, investments, insurance, property & loans, digital accounts, trusted contacts, **education documents** and **medical documents** — in English and हिन्दी.
- **Fill details from photo**: attach a photo of Aadhaar/PAN and the app reads the number and dates on the device and fills them in for you to check — the photo never leaves the phone.
- **Document photos** stay encrypted on the device and print at the back of your dossier.
- **Printable Family Emergency Dossier (PDF)** — filled, or blank to complete by hand. Print it, seal it, keep it with your will or in a locker.
- Passphrase + fingerprint unlock, masked account numbers, automatic re-lock, one-tap erase.

### ⚠️ Please read (trial-build honesty)
- This is a **debug trial build**. Each trial release is signed differently, so installing a newer trial requires **uninstalling the old one first — which erases everything entered in the app.** Please don't treat this trial as your permanent copy yet; the signed release (updates without uninstalling) comes before any wide public launch.
- There is deliberately **no backup and no recovery backdoor**: if the phone is lost or the passphrase forgotten, the dossier inside the app is gone. Write your passphrase on paper and seal it with your will; the printed PDF is your family's sealed copy.
- **Never type passwords, UPI/ATM PINs, OTPs or CVVs into the app.** Record *where the password is kept*, never the password itself.
